# Play Store listing

Use this copy for the Mixology store listing. Capture screenshots on a Pixel-class phone at the default font scale, then again at 1.3× and 2.0× to confirm TalkBack labels and wrapping.

## English

**Short description**
Browse, search, and save cocktail recipes. Swipe Randomixer for something new.

**Full description**
Mixology is a cocktail browser powered by TheCocktailDB.

- Hot picks and a full catalog of categories, glasses, ingredients, and alcoholic types
- Search by name, ingredient, or first letter
- Randomixer: swipe to save or skip, with a local deck and undo
- Saved recipes with notes, a shopping list, and an optional video link
- Home-screen widget for saved cocktails
- Light, dark, and system appearance

Recipes are for adults of legal drinking age. Drink responsibly.

## Español

**Descripción breve**
Explora, busca y guarda recetas de cócteles. Desliza en Randomixer para probar algo nuevo.

**Descripción completa**
Mixology es un explorador de cócteles con datos de TheCocktailDB.

- Destacados y un catálogo de categorías, vasos, ingredientes y tipos alcohólicos
- Búsqueda por nombre, ingrediente o primera letra
- Randomixer: desliza para guardar o saltar, con cola local y deshacer
- Recetas guardadas con notas, lista de la compra y vídeo opcional
- Widget de cócteles guardados
- Apariencia clara, oscura o del sistema

Las recetas son para adultos en edad legal. Bebe con responsabilidad.
