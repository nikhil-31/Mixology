Mixology is a free cocktail browser for iPhone. Browse, search, and save recipes from TheCocktailDB, including Randomixer, a shopping list, and saved drinks.

FAQ

Where do recipes come from?
Recipe names, ingredients, and instructions come from TheCocktailDB. Mixology does not create the recipes.

Is my data stored on my phone?
Saved drinks, notes, shopping list items, and bar ingredients stay on your device. Mixology does not create an account.

Why do I see ads?
The app shows a Google AdMob banner. You can review ad privacy choices in Settings when your region requires them.

Who is Mixology for?
Recipes are for adults of legal drinking age. Drink responsibly.
